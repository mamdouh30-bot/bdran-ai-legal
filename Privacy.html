Privacy Policy for BDRAN AI

Last updated: November 9, 2026

BDRAN AI is a global AI news channel (@bdran.ai) that publishes daily AI news videos in English on TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in with TikTok via Login Kit, we receive your basic public profile information provided by TikTok (username, profile photo) only to authenticate you as the creator of @bdran.ai. We do not collect passwords.

When you visit our TikTok channel, TikTok itself may collect analytics (views, likes). We do not collect personal data from viewers.

2. How We Use Information

We use the authenticated account only to publish AI news videos via TikTok Content Posting API on behalf of the creator, after the creator authorizes once.

3. Cookies

Our pages do not use tracking cookies. TikTok's platform may use its own cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

We use TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. Please review TikTok's Privacy Policy for how TikTok handles your data.

5. Data Retention

We keep the access token only as long as needed to publish videos. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok Settings > Security > Authorized Apps.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy question, contact us via TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/@bdran.ai
